# Workflow Readiness Gate

- [ ] Scope, trigger and outcome are explicit.
- [ ] Current state, policy and future-state proposal are separated.
- [ ] Every actor, system, handoff and exception has evidence or an explicit gap.
- [ ] Every step has a unique id and accountable owner.
- [ ] Dependencies are known, acyclic and deterministic.
- [ ] Every step validates as AEXOS Task Contract V1.
- [ ] Produced paths have one owner and no conflict.
- [ ] Agentic and external steps declare required capabilities.
- [ ] Side effects and human approvals are classified as governed.
- [ ] Failure, timeout, retry, idempotency and rollback expectations are recorded where applicable.
- [ ] QA was performed independently from design repair.
- [ ] The artifact does not claim execution occurred.
